Barq 27B — policy 4.0, 2026-10-06

Barq 27B is a derivative serving distribution based on Prism ML's Ternary Bonsai 2
27B GGUF, based on Qwen3.8-27B according to the upstream model card. Original
weight authorship remains with the upstream authors. Numerical tensor weights
were not trained or requantized in this project.

Barq contributions: original compact operating policy, embedded chat-template
wrapper, GGUF display/operating metadata, six-mode Chat Completions gateway,
FIFO admission, launch/integration files and scoped external-memory bridge.
Packaging and project maintained by D0shi-Eng. No affiliation with Prism ML,
Anthropic or OpenAI is implied, and no parity with their models is claimed.
The policy is original Barq text; third-party leaked prompts are not distributed.
